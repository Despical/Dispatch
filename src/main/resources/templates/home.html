A few things to know about Dispatch.

Have another question?
Does Dispatch replace my email provider?

No. Dispatch connects to your existing mail accounts so you can read and send email from one workspace. Your provider still hosts your mailbox.

Which accounts can I connect?

Gmail connects through Google authorization when the workspace administrator has configured it. Other accounts can connect through IMAP and SMTP over TLS; some providers require an app password.

Can I share selected mailboxes?

Yes. Choose a person with a Dispatch account, set their permissions, and select the mailboxes to share. Viewing, sending, organizing, and moving mail to Trash are separate permissions. You can change or remove access later.

What happens when I disconnect a mailbox?

Disconnecting removes the local connection and its stored credential. It does not delete your account or messages at your email provider.
